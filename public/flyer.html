Open Farm Tools

Local first farm tools for the next generation of farmers

Smart technology for all farmers, not just Big Ag.

Smart water valves

Open and close a tap from your phone. It measures the real water flow and tells you if something is off.

Livestock geofence trackers

A GPS collar that alerts you when an animal leaves its area, and helps you find it if it gets lost.

Automated chicken doors

A mechanical door with sensors that confirm it is closed and locked for the night.
